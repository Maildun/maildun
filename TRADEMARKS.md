# Maildun trademark policy

The code is licensed under the AGPL with additional terms (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The name "Maildun" and the Maildun logo are not: term 3 of the additional terms grants no rights under trademark law to them. This policy explains how you may use the name and logo anyway. It is separate from the copyright license, and nothing here limits what that license lets you do with the code.

## Uses that need no permission

- **Running Maildun.** Run an unmodified release or official build under its own name and logo, for yourself or as a hosted service for others.
- **Redistributing Maildun.** Share unmodified copies, including the official Docker images, under the Maildun name.
- **Minor local changes.** Configuration, theming through built-in settings, and backported security fixes that do not change what the software does keep the name.
- **Accurate references.** Say that your work is "based on Maildun", "a fork of Maildun", or "compatible with Maildun". Keep the default "Powered by Maildun" link and the About page attribution.

## Uses that need a different name

- **Modified versions.** If you distribute a modified version, or offer one to people outside your organization, give it its own name and logo. Neither may be confusingly similar to Maildun's. You may still describe it accurately as based on Maildun.
- **Implied endorsement.** Do not use the name or logo in a way that suggests your product, service, or company is official, endorsed by, or affiliated with Maildun.
- **Your own names.** Do not use "Maildun", or anything confusingly similar, in your company, product, or domain names.
