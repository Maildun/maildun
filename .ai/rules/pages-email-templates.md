# Pages Email Templates

## Templates are a first-class sidebar item
Templates live in the app sidebar next to Campaigns and Transactional. Starters remain team-less read-only rows; scoped {emailTemplate} bindings 404 on edit/update/destroy, and teams duplicate a starter into their library to compose it.
